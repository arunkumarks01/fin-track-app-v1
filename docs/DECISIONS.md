| DEC-1B | Intraday storage + derivation | **1-minute is the single stored intraday source**, in a compact hypertable `app.ohlcv_1m` (int4 paisa, smallint **local** id from the stable `app.instrument_ids` map — not Kite's bigint token, ~90 B/row). Live and backtest **must** use this function (no pandas resample). Golden-tested to reproduce Kite's native 5m/15m/30m/60m bars **exactly**. **1d stays Kite's native daily bar** (NSE's official close ≠ the last 1m close). Completeness is **calendar-aware**: expected minutes = the exchange benchmark's count that session (NIFTY 50 / SENSEX), so short sessions are flagged for review, never auto-quarantined. A materialized hot window is the escape hatch **only if** on-read latency hurts. Supersedes the "scheduled rollup job" fallback (2026-11-19) |
| DEC-1B-CAL | Intraday session calendar (**revised 2026-11-20 — supersedes DEC-1B's "expected minutes = the exchange benchmark's count"**) | **Per instrument, never cross-sectional.** Each session is judged against the instrument's **own** norm: the mode of its first `INTRADAY_NORM_BASELINE_SESSIONS`, re-baselined only on a **persistent step-change** (a new length held `INTRADAY_NORM_STEP_SESSIONS` = 10 consecutive sessions becomes the norm from its first session — the 2026 closing-auction switch 375→360 is a new normal, a week-long feed gap is not). A **market-wide short day** (≥ `INTRADAY_MARKET_SHORT_SHARE` of an exchange's judged instruments, at least `INTRADAY_MARKET_MIN_INSTRUMENTS`, below their own norm — outage, muhurat, half-day) makes those short sessions `market_short`: reported, not flagged. Indices against their own norm (no percentile over 4 symbols). Stored per session in `app.intraday_session_status`. Why: the benchmark's own series is gappy (NIFTY 50 = 54 minutes on the 2021-02-24 outage), and the **closing auction** (SEBI circular HO/47/11/11(3)2025-MRD-POD2/I/2765/2026, 16 Jan 2026 — identified, text not yet read; see CHANGELOG) rolled out per instrument (206 equities on 2026-08-03, then 3/3/6 on 08-26/09-30/10-28). `exchange_sessions` still supplies each session's **open** (time_bucket origin); its `expected_minutes` column is no longer used for completeness. |
| DEC-1C-FINAL | Daily bar finality (2026-11-20) | Kite publishes a session's **official** daily close overnight; a 1d bar fetched the same evening is provisional (2026-11-19: 223/224 closes changed by 08:05 next day). Session D's 1d bar is fetched only from **D+1 `DAILY_BAR_FINAL_NEXT_DAY_IST` (08:15)**; the daily job runs `30 8 * * 1-6`. 1m bars are final the same evening (`INTRADAY_FINAL_AFTER_IST`, verified identical next morning). Every stored daily close is cross-checked against NSE's official close (a newest window ≠ 1 is `official_mismatch`). The 224 provisional bars of 2026-11-19 were repaired through the audited path (`app.data_repairs` + quarantine `superseded_provisional`). |
| DEC-B | Corporate actions + adjustment (**revised 2026-11-20, Phase 1c — supersedes "Kite primary / model the adjustment" AND the interim "factor = Kite ÷ NSE ratio"**) | **Measure, don't model — and the measured map is AFFINE.** Kite publishes no corporate-actions list; its candles arrive already adjusted, more richly than NSE's action list, for part of history by **subtracting** dividends (ASHOKLEY 2018: Kite = 0.4998·NSE − ₹2.45; TATASTEEL 2022: 0.9772·NSE − ₹3.52), and not always alike across grains (RELIANCE 1m/daily = 0.991 until the 2020-05-13 rights ex-date). So: **adjusted view = Kite's data stored unchanged**; **as-traded daily = NSE's official bhavcopy** (exact); **Kite = a·NSE + b, measured per window** with a **minimax** fit whose worst residual over every open/high/low/close point must be ≤ **half the window's measured Kite price grid, never below `ADJ_TOLERANCE_PAISA`** (±2.5 on a 5-paisa grid; ±5 where every price in the window sits on a 10-paisa grid and there are ≥ `ADJ_MIN_WINDOW_DAYS`×4 prices to prove it — approved 2026-11-20; each point is checked against its own window's bound) — exceeding it never yields an accepted fit (the day is a flagged blip or starts a new window); windows are forced at NSE ex-dates, **a and b may step only at a matching NSE action** (an unmatched step is flagged: badge if small, suspect if large → no as-traded derived from either side); windows with fewer than `ADJ_MIN_WINDOW_DAYS` measured days are `underdetermined` (flagged, no as-traded) unless their points fit a neighbour's line; the 1m grain (G) and volume (V, scaled only for share-count events) get their own measured ratios; **as-traded 1m = (Kite ÷ G − b) ÷ a** (golden-tested inverse), NULL wherever the day isn't usable. Kite re-adjusting history later is caught by the **daily drift re-check** (newest stored bar re-fetched each catch-up; consistent rescaling → `adjustment_drift`; stored bars never rewritten; no check → "may be stale"). Source: NSE's **static archive** (`nsearchives.nseindia.com` PR zips: `Bc` actions + `Pd` official prices) with an honest User-Agent — **never impersonate a browser** (`www.nseindia.com` 403s non-browsers). Every zip's member files must carry the requested date (NSE's `PR020118.zip` holds 2019-01-02 files → refused, recorded as a gap) and the prev-close continuity check must hold. Each day's NSE names are resolved to our current symbol via NSE's `symbolchange.csv` (renames, e.g. IIFLWAM→360ONE), and the official row is EQ, else trade-for-trade BE, else BZ (new listings' first sessions); a parser-version bump re-scans older days once, inserting only missing rows. |

## Phase-10 forward-test gate (locked — tune #2/#5 to appetite)
All must hold before live: (1) ≥100 closed paper trades, ≥20/strategy, ≥4 wks, profitable ≥3
of 4 wks; (2) expectancy **≥ +0.25R net of costs**, positive after dropping top 2 winners;
(3) profit factor ≥ 1.3; (4) win-rate within ±10pp of backtest (consistency, not an absolute
gate); (5) max drawdown ≤ 12% of paper capital; (6) modeled fill within ±25% of real printed
post-signal price, no systematic optimism; (7) forward expectancy ≥ 65% of backtested;
(8) zero unexplained orders/incidents, kill-switch + dead-man's-switch verified, 100%
reconciled; (9) small live pilot (min size, semi-auto) measures real slippage before full live.
**Win-rate is a cross-check, not the gate — expectancy is. Paper can't fully prove slippage —
hence the live pilot.**
